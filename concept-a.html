The profile

Miracle Echeji is a Nollywood actress and broadcaster. Audiences also know her as Mimi of Lagos. She comes from Mbaise in Imo State, and she built her presenting skills in broadcasting before she moved to the screen.

Her film work includes Someone Like You, Just A Friend and Compliance. She plays Ozege in Once Upon A Village.
