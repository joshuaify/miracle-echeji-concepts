Profile

Miracle Echeji is a Nollywood actress and broadcaster, known to audiences as Mimi of Lagos. She comes from Mbaise in Imo State and built her presenting skills in broadcasting before she moved to the screen.

On film she appears in Someone Like You, Just A Friend and Compliance. In Once Upon A Village she plays Ozege.
